## General Motivation

Cuspidal robots challenge the classical intuition that changing inverse-kinematics branch requires crossing a singularity. Understanding this phenomenon is essential for reliable execution, repeatability, and planning in modern manipulators.

This chapter will focus on the geometric and practical meaning of branch switching without singular crossing.
